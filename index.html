Who I Am:

I'm a self-taught programmer and 12th-grade student at Whiteland Community High School — originally from Karachi, now living in Greenwood, Indiana. I've been building with Python, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS since middle school, not for grades, but because I genuinely wanted to understand how things worked.

My long-term focus is machine learning and generative AI: fields where design decisions carry consequences far beyond any single system. I came to that understanding not from a textbook, but from an internship at a digital bank where I watched engineers reconcile software with religious principle.

Outside of code, I debate, compete in Model United Nations, and think a lot about the relationship between technology and the people it affects.
